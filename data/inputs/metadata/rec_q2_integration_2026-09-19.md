- Primary endpoint: atanh(rho[Claudin,HRC])-atanh(rho[Polarity,HRC]) in each patient. Study summaries equally weight patients, with 10,000 patient bootstraps and exhaustive sign flips. Pool study means and their between-patient variance/n using REML and Hartung-Knapp t inference (metafor); report heterogeneity and every leave-one-study analysis. Ogden is not pooled with validation studies. One primary external pooled component-difference test; individual-gene pooled tests constitute a separate BH family of 30. Two component correlations and score/model sensitivities are labelled secondary. A study with fewer than two finite patient estimates cannot supply a meta-analytic variance and is reported descriptively.
- Members use the same HRC residual and covariate design as the programme comparison, with log1p(CP10k) single-gene expression. Report per-patient detection and all 30 member estimates; constant residuals are missing, not zero. Gene effects do not select patients or studies.
- Quality checks: integer/nonnegative external raw counts, full-library accounting, unique cell IDs, no duplicated donor across study labels after original accession review, common memberships, explicit score changes relative to legacy Ogden, and independently reconstructed partial correlations and pooled estimates.
